## Reconstruction assumptions and limits
- This is a native reconstruction using supplied dimensions and rendered STL references, not an exact mesh trace. Unmeasured flange corner clips/radii, cone dimensions, seat spacing, and shoulder blends are editable estimates.
- The user reported a threaded end snapped during insertion **at the shoulder above the threads**. The exact print settings and whether the thread bound before fracture are not known. Both parts now use an earlier-starting 3-section shoulder: it begins at Z **8.50 mm**, has **5.10 mm radius** at Z **10.00 mm**, and transitions to **7.50 mm radius** at the upper section. This adds material around the unthreaded neck while retaining the existing helical pitch, crest and root dimensions. It is a proposed local reinforcement, not a demonstrated strength improvement or a repair to breaks within the thread itself; check socket clearance and test the printed connection.
- Modeled screw ends use **1.814 mm pitch**, **10.65 mm crest diameter**, four rounded helical turns and a relieved **9.56 mm core**. Pitch, crest diameter and rounding were checked against IEC 60061-1 sheet 7004-6-1; the relieved root/runout are a simplified first-fit reconstruction, not a gauged standard cap or an exact copy of the source thread.
- Thread handedness was reversed in the first reconstruction. Matched front-view close-ups confirmed the mismatch with the original 3bit STL. Both part files now use `ccw = false` with the +Z helix, and the new front-view winding matches the original. Thread pitch, diameters, rounding and turn count are unchanged. Boolean order was adjusted to execute the corrected winding without changing the intended shape.
- The user reports that the revised flared base is great and the thread now fits perfectly. This does not establish torque strength, fatigue life, electrical insulation or operating temperature. PETG snap force and the revised head's retention remain untested. Test with the fixture unpowered.

## Validation
- The edited blue part executes with **18 fully constrained sketches**; the existing gold part had **10**. The main comparison layout also executes.
- Reviewed whole-part and end-on views of the widened longitudinal opening and side supports. Requested openings are subtractions, not displayed cutter bodies.
- The current blue part's FDM mesh-integrity check completed on one component without an integrity finding. Sampled thickness/gap coverage was partial and no minimum requirements were supplied; the smallest sampled normal-ray thickness was **0.26 mm**, near the lower head/flare transition, not necessarily a uniform wall or the continuous minimum. The 0.16 mm +Z screening flagged overhangs at the flange, thread/neck, head, ledges and lips. It does not validate slicer supports, fit, torque, layer bonding, heat or electrical safety.
